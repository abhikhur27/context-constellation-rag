# VAT Policy Review — 2026-06-19

Compliance requires one VAT assessment per taxable line and prohibits collecting duplicate tax during checkout. The observed German and French invoices violate that rule even when authorization latency and service availability remain healthy.

Launch approval depends on a corrected calculation, evidence from the 500-order sample, and Finance confirmation that prior overcharges entered the refund workflow. Compliance has not approved European expansion yet.
