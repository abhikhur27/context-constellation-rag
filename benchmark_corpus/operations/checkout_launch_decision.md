# Checkout Launch Decision — 2026-06-18

Status: rollout paused for the European checkout cohort. The launch council paused expansion after duplicate VAT lines appeared on completed German and French invoices. This is a tax-correctness decision, not a capacity or latency decision.

The service passed load testing and remains inside the latency error budget. Resumption requires Finance to reconcile the affected invoices, Compliance to approve the corrected VAT calculation, and Support to confirm that new duplicate-tax cases have stopped. The current decision supersedes the May launch draft.
