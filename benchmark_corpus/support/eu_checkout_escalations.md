# EU Checkout Customer Escalations — 2026-06-18

Support received 37 reports from German and French customers whose completed checkout invoices contained duplicate VAT lines. The charged total matched the inflated invoice, so these are financial-correctness incidents rather than cosmetic receipt defects.

Support recommends keeping the European rollout paused until a fresh production sample contains no duplicate-tax cases. Search, login, and mobile-release complaints were unchanged during the same window.
