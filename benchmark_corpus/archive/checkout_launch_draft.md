# Checkout Launch Draft — 2026-05-28 — Superseded

This archived planning draft proposed opening the European checkout cohort to all traffic on June 20. At the time of writing, synthetic latency, payment authorization, and invoice snapshots were green, so the draft described the launch as ready to proceed.

This document predates the duplicate VAT incident and is superseded by the June 18 launch decision. It must not be used as the current go-live authority.
